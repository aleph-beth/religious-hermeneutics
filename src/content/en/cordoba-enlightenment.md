**Type**: Dossier opening — seventeen parts
**Thesis**: The European Enlightenment is not a spontaneous generation. The autonomy of reason, the criticism of sacred texts, the experimental method, the separation of knowledge from faith — the operations that define European modernity were first formulated in Arabic and Judeo-Arabic, in the Islamic world and above all in Cordoba, between the ninth and thirteenth centuries. Europe received them, naturalized them, and then turned them against the world that had produced them.

---

## 1. The standard account and what it leaves out {#s1}

The textbook story of the Enlightenment runs in a straight line: Greece invents reason, Rome transmits it, the Christian Middle Ages let it sleep, the Renaissance rediscovers it by returning *to the sources*, and the eighteenth century completes it. In that story the Arab-Muslim world appears only as a depository — a strongbox that kept the Greek manuscripts safe until Europe came to collect them. Renan gave the formula its canonical form: Islam was the librarian, never the author.

The account is wrong on three checkable points.

**First, the chronology.** The experimental method does not wait for Francis Bacon. It is formulated, applied and published by Ibn al-Haytham around 1020 — six centuries earlier. The pointed arch and the ribbed vault are not born at Saint-Denis in 1140: they are built at the Mezquita of Cordoba from the eighth century onward. Western Europe's first paper mill is not German but Andalusian, at Xàtiva, two centuries before Gutenberg.

**Second, the nature of the transfer.** What Europe receives is not a stock of intact Greek texts. It is a corpus reworked, extended, corrected — and, crucially, delivered together with *methods*. Averroes does not recopy Aristotle, he comments on him; and it is the commentary, not the bare text, that becomes compulsory at the University of Paris in 1255. Al-Khwarizmi does not preserve Indian calculation, he turns it into algebra. What is transferred are intellectual operations, not documents.

**Third, the language itself testifies.** Algebra, algorithm, cipher, zero, alcohol, alkali, alembic, azimuth, zenith, nadir, almanac, admiral, arsenal, tariff, customs, magazine, cotton, sugar, syrup, saffron, lute, guitar — Europe's technical, commercial, scientific and musical vocabulary is Arabic. A civilization that had merely kept books does not leave its words inside the trades of the civilization that reads them.

## 2. The three phases of the mechanism {#s2}

The dossier is organized around a single pattern, traced through every part: **reception, expulsion, conquest**.

| Phase | Period | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| **Reception** | 12th–13th centuries | Europe translates, absorbs and integrates Andalusian knowledge — Toledo, Palermo, Montpellier, Paris |
| **Expulsion** | 14th–16th centuries | It physically expels the bearers of that knowledge: 1492 for the Jews, 1609 for the Moriscos — and erases their names from the account |
| **Conquest** | 18th–20th centuries | It deploys the naturalized inheritance — navigation, artillery, medicine, cartography, administration — to colonize the lands it came from |

This is not a rhetorical irony. It is a documentable sequence, and each part of the dossier follows one thread through it: philosophy, mysticism, optics, medicine, paper, agriculture, music, architecture, navigation, gunpowder.

## 4. What this dossier does not claim {#s4}

Inverting the standard account easily produces an inverse account that is just as false. Four guardrails.

**There is no essence.** Neither an "Arab genius" nor a "European genius." What produces tenth-century Cordoba — concentrated capital, relative tolerance, manuscript circulation, patronage, multilingualism — are historical conditions, and they come undone: Cordoba collapses in 1031, *falsafa* is marginalized by Ash'arism, printing in Arabic characters is refused until the eighteenth century. The same causes, running the other way, explain Europe's rise. On this point see [Maimonides, the Spinoza that the Islamic World Never Had?](../maimonides-spinoza/).

**Transmission is not the whole story.** Receiving Averroes was not sufficient: Europe did something with him that the Islamic world did not, and that difference needs a historical explanation of its own — it is in fact the knot of the dossier.

**Cordoba is not alone.** Baghdad, Cairo, Kairouan, Isfahan and Samarkand matter as much; Byzantium and Italy have channels of their own. "Cordoba" names here a particularly well-documented crossing point, not a single source. The [chronology of encounters](../islam-europe-chronology/) places the full network.

**Al-Andalus was not a paradise.** *Convivencia* was an unequal legal regime punctuated by persecution — the Almohads exiled Averroes exactly as they exiled Maimonides. This dossier studies an intellectual transmission, not a lost utopia.

## 5. Why it matters {#s5}

The debt was not forgotten through absent-mindedness. It was erased at the precise moment Europe set about colonizing the Muslim world: one does not comfortably conquer a civilization one knows oneself to be the heir of. Concealment is the ideological precondition of conquest — the subject of [The Concealment of the Debt](../debt-concealment/).

Restoring the genealogy is therefore not symbolic reparation. It corrects an error of description: the Enlightenment is not the property of a civilization but the outcome of a long shared labor, carried out in Arabic, Hebrew, Judeo-Arabic, Latin and Castilian, by people who claimed none of them in particular.
